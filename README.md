### General Impressions & UI/UX
Hey Group 1! I just spent some time testing out the GameMatch project, and honestly, I’m really impressed with what you guys built. First off, the concept itself is awesome—trying to find decent people to play games with is an actual struggle, so the use-case is incredibly valid for our demographic.

Looking at the UI/UX, you completely nailed the gaming aesthetic. A lot of gaming-focused apps fall into the trap of being way too cluttered or overly flashy, but your layout is really clean. It balances the theme perfectly without sacrificing readability, which is exactly the kind of stuff we’ve been talking about in our design lectures. 

### Core Functionality & Mobile Testing
I tested the core functionality, specifically the filtering and matching logic, and everything felt super snappy. When I clicked around to find matches, the results updated exactly how I expected. 

I also made sure to test the site on my phone because I know mobile responsiveness is a huge part of our grading rubric. You guys did a fantastic job with the CSS grid system. The cards and images scaled down flawlessly to fit my screen, and nothing looked stretched, squished, or broken—which is usually the hardest part of getting flexbox and grid layouts right!

### Constructive Feedback
For my testing feedback, I have two small suggestions that could take the polish to the next level before final grading:

1. **Loading States:** When I click to generate a match or load a page with heavier images, there is a split second where it feels like the screen is completely frozen. Adding a simple loading spinner or a skeleton loader would be a massive UX improvement. It lets the user know the system is actually working in the background and prevents them from rage-clicking.
2. **Empty States:** It might be worth checking what happens when a search fails. If a user applies a bunch of highly specific filters and nobody matches, make sure there's a friendly *"No matches found, try clearing your filters!"* message instead of just a blank empty screen. 

Overall though, this is a really solid web app and you guys should be proud of the final result. Awesome work!
